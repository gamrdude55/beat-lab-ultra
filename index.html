Beat Lab Ultra

Make beats. Tap sounds. Build your own neon sound.

🎛️ Studio controls

Ready. Press Play or tap a pad. Your browser may ask to enable audio after the first click.

🎚️ 16-step sequencer

Click squares to switch sounds on/off. Each row is one sound; the bright outline shows the current step.

🥁 Drum & bass pads

Tap a pad to play it instantly. Headphones at a comfortable volume can make the details easier to hear.

🎹 Melody sequencer

Tap note squares to toggle a short 8-step melody. Notes play with the beat and can be changed anytime.
